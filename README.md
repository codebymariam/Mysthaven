## Enter the manor

Mysthaven is a gothic social-deduction game for **4–8 players**. Explore a candlelit manor, solve room puzzles, face supernatural threats, and decide who to trust before the next vote.

| Rooms | Tasks | Characters | Rewards |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **15** | **33** | **9** | **76** |

## Gameplay

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Explore the manor** <br> Move through 15 interconnected rooms and use the map to track your location. | **Complete tasks** <br> Light chandeliers, decode runes, and repair the manor’s machinery. |
| **Discover characters** <br> Choose a character at the lobby mirror and use their ability to help your side. | **Survive the threats** <br> Watch for Veil enemies, report discoveries, and decide whom to eject. |

## HCI design

Developed for **COSC 452**, Mysthaven uses visible task references, direct manipulation, vote confirmation, and feedback for role changes, cooldowns, and results.

| Formative study, version 3 | Result |
| --- | ---: |
| Participants | 5 |
| Applicable task attempts | 22 |
| Completed independently | 20/22 (90.9%) |
| Mean ease rating | 1.05/5 (1 = very easy) |
